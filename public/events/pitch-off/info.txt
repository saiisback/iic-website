PITCH OFF
ABOUT THE EVENT
PITCH OFF was an engaging, fast-paced event conducted by BICEP-IIC, designed to challenge participants' creativity, problem-solving, and presentation skills under time constraints. Held on 27th May 2025, from 11:30 AM to 4:30 PM, at the Seminar Hall, Academic Block, 2nd Floor, the event attracted enthusiastic participation from 20 teams, all vying to deliver the most compelling pitch across multiple competitive rounds.With spontaneous problem-solving, critical thinking, and real-time cross-questioning, the event provided an excellent platform for students to showcase their entrepreneurial flair and communication prowess. To support the participants during the intensive sessions, refreshments were thoughtfully provided.







                                                       PITCH OFF 





ROUND 1: INSTANT PITCH CHALLENGE
The competition kicked off with 20 teams. Each team randomly picked a chit containing a unique problem statement. They were given 10 minutes to assess the problem, devise a solution, and prepare a 2.5-minute pitch, followed by answers to 3 related questions. The pitches were evaluated by judges Reyyan and Kushal based on criteria like clarity, creativity, and feasibility.


After careful evaluation, the top 10 teams were selected for the next round based on:
Uniqueness of idea


Quality of presentation


Feasibility and clarity


ROUND 2: CROSS-PITCHING 
In this round, the 10 shortlisted teams were divided into two groups: Group A and Group B. Each group was assigned 5 unique problem statements, with two teams receiving the same statement within each group. Teams had to develop a comprehensive pitch using a PowerPoint presentation and then engage in cross-questioning with the other team that had the same problem.
Each group was given 15 minutes to prepare and deliver their pitch. This round emphasized:
Depth of understanding


Team collaboration


Critical thinking during cross-questioning


The round was evaluated by Sai Karthik, Shrisha, and Swatantra, who assessed the teams on their clarity of ideas, presentation quality, and their ability to respond confidently to challenges raised during cross-questioning.






FINAL ROUND: DETAILED PITCH & Q&A SHOWDOWN
The top 6 teams from Round 2 advanced to the final round. Each team was given 20 minutes to prepare their final pitch and 4.5 minutes to present it. Teams had to pitch their solutions in greater detail, tackling questions from both the judges and opposing teams. This round tested their:
Concept depth and refinement


Defense under scrutiny


Confidence and adaptability


The high level of competition and intellectual rigor was evident as teams presented refined pitches and demonstrated their ability to think on their feet.
This round was evaluated by Sai Karthik, Swatantra, Shrisha, and Prof. Shama H M, who challenged participants with incisive questions and assessed them on their technical soundness, articulation, and ability to defend their ideas under pressure.
