

BULLISH- THE MARKET-FIRST HACKATHON REPORT
About the Hackathon :
Bullish is a 48-hour market-first hackathon hosted by BMS Institute of Technology & Management (BMSIT&M) in collaboration with BICEP and the Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC) , held on 20th and 21st of May 2026. Unlike traditional hackathons focused only on product development, Bullish emphasized market validation, user acquisition, and product visibility through real-world execution.
The event combined an on-campus kickoff, a remote execution sprint, and an on-campus finale, enabling participants to develop, launch, and market their solutions while gaining exposure to entrepreneurial skills such as growth strategy, customer validation, and rapid execution.



Competition Structure :
Phase 1: Kickoff Session (On-Campus)
The first phase of the hackathon marked the official launch of the event and served as the foundation for the execution sprint. Participants were introduced to the objectives, rules, judging criteria, and overall structure of the competition through a detailed orientation session. 
Following the orientation, teams finalized their problem statements and officially began the 48-hour hackathon journey.
This phase ensured that all participants were aligned with the expectations of the competition and prepared for the intensive product development and marketing stages ahead.
Statistics:
Teams Registered: 32
Teams Participated: 14

Phase 2: Remote Build & Market Sprint
(48-Hour Remote Sprint)
Phase 2 constituted the core execution stage of the hackathon, where teams worked remotely to develop and launch their Minimum Viable Products (MVPs) while acquiring real users within the sprint duration. Teams promoted their solutions through various digital outreach channels, while a centralized leaderboard tracked real-time engagement and user acquisition metrics. 
Mentors and organizers remained available throughout the phase to provide technical and strategic guidance. The phase emphasized execution capability, market validation, and user acquisition alongside technical innovation.

Phase 3: Finale and Closing Ceremony (On-Campus)
The final phase of the hackathon was conducted on campus and focused on product presentations, metric verification, evaluation, and recognition of the winning teams. Teams showcased their product journey, market strategy, user acquisition efforts, and overall impact achieved during the sprint.
Tracking links and leaderboard metrics were verified prior to evaluation to ensure transparency and fairness in the judging process. The event concluded with an awards ceremony recognizing the top-performing teams for their innovation, execution, and market reach.
The ceremony was graced by the presence of Dr. Raju Hajare and Prof. Shama H M, whose continuous guidance and support played a significant role in the successful execution of the hackathon.

Participation and Engagement :
To enhance participant engagement and create an energetic atmosphere throughout the event, several interactive activities were conducted alongside the hackathon sessions. Activities such as the “67 Challenge,” Push-Up Challenge, and Scream Decibel Challenge encouraged active participation, healthy competition, and audience involvement. Winners were determined based on performance metrics including activity count, endurance, and measured decibel levels. These engagement activities contributed significantly to maintaining enthusiasm and fostering a vibrant event environment among participants.
