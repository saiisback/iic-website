           

                   



                             ANVESHANA BOOTCAMP 
IIC BMSIT 
            11-01-2025 
Event Coordinator:
Prof. Shama H M
The boot camp, conducted by Mr. Suhruth G Ujjni, a seasoned entrepreneur and dynamic leader, aimed to equip aspiring entrepreneurs with critical knowledge and practical tools to navigate the startup ecosystem.
Suhruth G Ujjni is a prominent figure in the entrepreneurial ecosystem. With a blend of technical expertise and strategic vision, he has spearheaded numerous initiatives to foster startup growth. Highlights of his career include: 
Founding Startup Tumakuru to support local startups in idea validation, fundraising, and overcoming operational challenges. 
Heading the CIF Rajajinagar at K-tech Innovation Hub, where he facilitated over 210 crore INR in funding for 220 startups within five years. 
Promoting Estonia's e-Residency program, helping 350 entrepreneurs establish businesses internationally. 
Expertise in building partnerships with government and educational institutions to enhance entrepreneurial outreach. 
Mr. Suhruth’s remarkable contributions underline his ability to drive innovation and nurture entrepreneurial ventures. 
The boot camp covered a comprehensive range of topics essential for budding entrepreneurs: 
What is Entrepreneurship? – Understanding the essence and scope of entrepreneurship. 
Key Qualities of an Entrepreneur – Traits necessary for entrepreneurial success. 
How Entrepreneurs Differ from Businessmen – A comparative analysis of mindset and approach. 
Understanding Value Proposition – Crafting unique value to address customer needs. 
Creating a Customer Profile – Identifying and segmenting target customers. 
Defining the Total Addressable Market (TAM) – Estimating the potential market size. 
Exploring the Business Model Canvas – Visualizing and strategizing business components. 
Calculating the Break-Even Point – Determining profitability thresholds. 
Go-to-Market Strategies – Designing effective market entry plans. 
Market Segmentation – Dividing the market into actionable segments. 
Understanding Unit Economics – Analyzing per-unit profitability. 
Introduction to Basic Financial Planning – Fundamentals of managing business finances. 
Types of Business Models – Exploring diverse frameworks for business operations. 
Key Takeaways 
Practical Knowledge: Participants gained actionable insights into building and scaling a startup. 
Strategic Frameworks: Tools like the Business Model Canvas provided a structured approach to entrepreneurship. 
Financial Literacy: Basics of financial planning and unit economics empowered participants to make informed decisions.

Morning Session Activities
The morning session of the bootcamp was dedicated to individual discussions .During these one-on-one interactions, he listened attentively to the teams' ideas, understanding their concepts, challenges, and goals. This personalized approach provided an invaluable opportunity for participants to gain insights tailored to their unique entrepreneurial journeys.
Mr. Suhruth offered specific guidance on various aspects of their ideas, such as refining their value propositions, identifying their target audiences, and exploring ways to scale their solutions effectively. The individualized nature of the discussions enabled each team to receive constructive feedback on the strengths of their proposals and actionable suggestions to overcome identified gaps or challenges.
Afternoon Session Activities
In the afternoon, the focus shifted to monitoring and enhancing the progress of the teams' Proof of Concept (PoC) developments. Mr. Suhruth reviewed each team's PoC in depth, providing a detailed evaluation of their prototypes. His hands-on approach ensured that the solutions were both technically viable and strategically aligned with market needs.
He also shared a range of reference materials, including industry reports, technical resources, and case studies, to help the teams strengthen their prototypes. These resources were specifically curated to address the teams' unique requirements, equipping them with the knowledge and tools needed to refine their PoCs further.
Additionally, Mr. Suhruth emphasized the importance of aligning their prototypes with customer expectations and real-world use cases. He encouraged teams to approach the development process iteratively, ensuring continuous improvement based on feedback and insights.


Broader Impact of the Sessions
Focused Mentorship: Teams greatly benefited from the personalized mentoring, which not only clarified their ideas but also boosted their confidence in pursuing their entrepreneurial endeavors.
Resource Enrichment: By providing targeted reference materials, Mr. Suhruth bridged knowledge gaps and empowered teams to address technical and strategic challenges effectively.
Prototype Validation and Optimization: The expert feedback and insights received during the PoC monitoring session enabled teams to enhance their prototypes' functionality, feasibility, and market readiness.
Skill Development: Participants gained practical skills in prototyping, problem-solving, and iterative development, which are critical for entrepreneurial success.
