ANVESHANA 3.0 – National Level Prototype       Competition Report
About the Competition :
Anveshana 3.0 is a flagship national-level prototyping competition hosted by BMS Institute of Technology & Management (BMSIT&M) in collaboration with BICEP (BMS Innovation Centre and Entrepreneurship Park) and the Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC).
Envisioned as a platform to nurture innovation and practical problem-solving, the competition brings together students from diverse academic backgrounds to transform ideas into functional prototypes. It emphasizes the application of technical knowledge to address real-world challenges, encouraging participants to think critically, design creatively, and execute effectively.
Structured across multiple progressive stages, Anveshana 3.0 provides participants with opportunities to refine their concepts through presentation, feedback, and hands-on development. The competition not only highlights technical excellence but also promotes collaboration, adaptability, and entrepreneurial thinking among students.

Competition Structure :
Round 1: Proposal Evaluation
The first round focused on the submission and screening of innovative project proposals. Participants presented their ideas in the form of structured abstracts outlining their concept, approach, and intended impact.
The round witnessed an exceptional response, reflecting the increasing interest among students in innovation-driven initiatives. Following a thorough review process, the most promising ideas were shortlisted for further development.
Statistic :
Initial Registrations: 1143
Teams Qualified for Round 2: 100

Round 2: Online PPT Presentation
(5th and 6th  of March) 

Round 2 was conducted  in an online format, where shortlisted teams presented their ideas and prototype concepts through structured PowerPoint presentations.
The round was organized over two days, with two sessions conducted each day, ensuring a smooth and systematic evaluation process. This format enabled effective time management while allowing participants to clearly communicate their concepts in a virtual setting.
The online mode facilitated wider participation and ensured accessibility for teams across different regions, further strengthening the national reach of the competition.

Round 3: Final Prototype Demonstration (Offline)
(27th March )
The final round of Anveshana 3.0 was conducted offline, marking the culmination of the competition. The top 50 shortlisted teams presented their working prototypes through dedicated stalls.
This round was designed to provide an immersive and interactive experience, enabling participants to demonstrate their innovations directly. The stall-based setup facilitated detailed discussions, allowing attendees and evaluators to engage closely with each project.
In addition to prototype displays, the event featured various engagement activities that contributed to a lively and collaborative atmosphere throughout the venue. A panel of four judges was responsible for evaluating the participating teams during this stage.

Participation and Engagement :
Anveshana 3.0 attracted participation from a large and diverse pool of students across the country. The competition served as a convergence point for innovative ideas spanning multiple domains, including technology, sustainability, healthcare, and smart systems.
The offline demonstration round significantly enhanced engagement by enabling real-time interaction between participants, peers, and organizers. This environment encouraged knowledge exchange, constructive discussions, and collaborative learning.
The inclusion of engagement activities further enriched the experience, making the event both intellectually stimulating and interactive for all attendees. 
      

Event Highlights :
Participation from over 1000+ registered teams across the country
Multi-stage structure enabling idea refinement and prototype development
Seamless integration of online and offline evaluation rounds
